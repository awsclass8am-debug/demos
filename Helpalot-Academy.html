Helpalot Pathway — How It Works

From Registration to Full-Time Job — a clear, structured journey.

1
Register & Apply

Fill the form, choose your program, pay the fee and submit your application.

2
Admin Review & Batch Allocation

Our team reviews your profile, verifies documents and places you in the right batch with a trainer.

3
Daily Live Classes (7 PM)

Attend live sessions every day. Attendance auto-marked. Recordings uploaded within 2 hours.

4
Assignments, Tests & Projects

Trainer assigns tasks weekly. Submit via your dashboard. Marks and feedback within 24 hours.

5
Internship (Real Work)

Work on actual Helpalot or partner company projects with weekly mentor review.

6
Certificate & Placement

Complete program → auto-generated certificate with QR code → placement support begins.
